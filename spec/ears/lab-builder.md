# Lab builder

| Id       | Pattern | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| STU-0123 | event   | When a lecturer adds a step to a lab by its title, the Studio shall propose a plan that writes the next numbered step page, titled as typed, at the end of the lab.                |
| STU-0124 | event   | When a lecturer moves a lab step earlier or later, the Studio shall propose a plan that renumbers the steps in their new order, keeping every page as it is.                       |
| STU-0125 | event   | When a lecturer renames or removes a lab step, the Studio shall propose a plan that renames the page and its heading, or moves it to the bin and renumbers the steps after it.     |
| STU-0126 | event   | When a lecturer chooses a picture for a lab step, the Studio shall propose a plan that copies it into the lab's img folder under a plain name and shows it at the end of the step. |
