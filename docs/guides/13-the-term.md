# 13. Running the term

Once a course has a start date (under **Course details**, **Teaching starts**), Studio follows the term with you.

## This term

The course home opens with **This term**. It says which teaching week it is, such as "This is week 3 of the term: Forms", which weeks are hidden from students and when each is due out.

## Keeping a week back until a day

Open a week at the top of the course. Under **Students see this week from**, choose the day and press **Keep it hidden until then**, then **Make this change**. Studio hides the week and keeps the day beside it in `properties.yaml`. Tutors only hides weeks and units at the top of a course, so only they have this.

**Take the date away** keeps the week as it is (hidden or not) and forgets the day.

## When a week is due

Studio doesn't change your course on its own, and students only see a change once you publish. When a week's day comes, **This term** says so, with **Show Week 4 to students**. Press it, then **Make this change**, then publish the course as usual (see [Check and publish](08-check-and-publish.md)).

## Starting next year

On the course home, open **Start next year from this one**.

1. Check the name. Studio suggests this one's with the year moved on.
2. Optionally give the first day of teaching. The calendar starts on that week's Monday.
3. Press **Make next year's course** and choose where to put its folder.

Studio makes a new course folder with the same weeks, content and outcomes, no release dates and nothing built, and opens it. This year's course stays exactly as it was.

## How students used each week

Studio will show, beside each week on the course home, how many students opened it and how long they spent, counts only and never names. It needs Tutors to let Studio read its time data first, so for now nothing shows there.
